CRIMEFILE / CASE 002 / DIGITAL EXHIBIT 03

Knowledge, Action, Intent

The evidence now suggests that Naomi received information about a serious medication discrepancy. But investigators must distinguish three different questions: what she knew, what she did, and what she intended.

Record 1 — Delivery receipt
Naomi’s work account received the medication discrepancy alert at 21:26. The receipt proves delivery to the account, not that she read or understood it.
Record 2 — Calendar audit
The audit shows Naomi moved Adrian’s 08:30 call to a later date after the alert was delivered. The audit does not, by itself, establish why she changed it.
Record 3 — Recovered draft
A draft in Naomi’s work account reads: “I saw the discrepancy. Do not wake him or call the clinic tonight. If this is raised tomorrow, I can say I never saw the alert.” The draft is timestamped after the alert. Investigators have not yet authenticated its author.

Question: Which conclusions are supported by the combined records? Select every defensible answer.

Design note: the recovered draft is a proposed clue. Use it only if you want the digital game to provide direct evidence of knowing inaction; otherwise replace it with a less conclusive record.
